# Unit 2: Selector Kernels

Purpose:
- formalize candidate selection, feasibility, optimality, and tie-breaking
- bridge from traces/lists into routing and strategy-style correctness

Why it matters:
- introduces a new proof family beyond invariant preservation
- connects directly to quote selection, routers, and search procedures

Typical theorem families:
- chosen result is feasible
- chosen result comes from the candidate set
- chosen result is at least as good as every valid candidate
