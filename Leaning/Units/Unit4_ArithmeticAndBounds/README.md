# Unit 4: Arithmetic and Bounds

Purpose:
- strengthen monotonicity/boundedness reasoning
- introduce the arithmetic/cast/tactic surface needed for later protocol math
- keep the focus on protocol use, not abstract algebra for its own sake

Why it matters:
- later AMM, controller, and risk units need stronger arithmetic fluency than
  the early `Nat`-only exercises

Typical theorem families:
- range theorems
- monotonicity
- threshold/clip behavior
- normalization and cast-management lemmas
